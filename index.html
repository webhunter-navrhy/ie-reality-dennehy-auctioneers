For Sale by Private Treaty.

Dennehy Auctioneers are delighted to present this rare opportunity to acquire a truly exceptional, virtually new architect designed coastal residence, situated in one of Cork’s most sought after locations. Designed and constructed to the very highest standards, this impressive home showcases superb finishes and meticulous attention to detail throughout, offering the quality, style and presentation of a showhouse property. The outstanding setting is complemented by spectacular panoramic views across Cork’s inner harbour, encompassing Spike Island, Cobh and its iconic Cathedral, together with Curraghbinny Headland. The ever changing vista is truly captivating, providing a stunning backdrop by day and an equally enchanting panorama by night. Combining exceptional architectural design, luxurious finishes and an unrivalled coastal setting, this is a rare opportunity to acquire a home of distinction in a highly desirable location.

Outside:

The beautifully landscaped gardens are a truly exceptional feature of this impressive home. The private rear garden is enclosed by striking natural stone walls and has been thoughtfully designed to create a superb outdoor sanctuary, perfectly suited to both relaxed family living and elegant entertaining. An extensive flagstone patio flows seamlessly onto a lush lawn and a superb PVC-decked area, complete with a stylish pergola illuminated by festoon lighting, creating a magical setting for al fresco dining and summer evenings. A wonderful array of mature shrubs, established planting and beautifully curated borders provides an abundance of colour, texture and privacy throughout the seasons. Convenient side access, with attractive tiled steps, connects the rear garden to the front of the property. To the front, an expansive cobblelock driveway and generous turning area provide excellent off-street parking, complemented by a raised flower bed framed by beautifully capped stone walls. An EV charging point further adds to the home’s modern credentials and convenience.
